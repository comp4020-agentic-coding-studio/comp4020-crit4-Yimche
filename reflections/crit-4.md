# Crit 2 — Unsolicited redesign

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?
The biggest breakthrough for me this week was when performing testing I
realised I was wasting a lot of time just clicking buttons in writing the music
for the orchestra to do testing.
I resolved this by creating dev testing tools: a way to load pre-made tracks
onto the orchestra, and save them, such that I could quickly make a tune, see
results, make changes, reload the page and put that same track back into the
system.

This helped a lot and I think led to me catching an unexpected behaviour / bug
I otherwise wouldn't have noticed: The musicians are stationary if they play 
every beat, as the only bob up when they play, and bob back down when they stop.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?
I think as a software developer this week's crit demonstrated to me why having
a method to test is very important, as without the ability to consistenly test
I was using a lot more time and was also unable to fully understand / capture
what was actually being written and the "expected / intended" behaviour of the
agentic coding tool.
